# VPN Setup and Troubleshooting Guide

## 1. Overview

A Virtual Private Network (VPN) allows employees to securely access company resources when working remotely or outside the office network.

Employees must connect to the company's approved VPN before accessing internal systems that require a secure connection.

## 2. Requirements

Before connecting to the company VPN, ensure that:

- You have a stable internet connection.
- The company-approved VPN client is installed.
- You have valid company login credentials.
- Your device meets the company's security requirements.

## 3. VPN Connection Procedure

1. Open the company-approved VPN application.
2. Enter the VPN server address provided by the IT department.
3. Enter your company username and password.
4. Complete multi-factor authentication (MFA), if requested.
5. Click Connect.
6. Wait until the application displays Connected.

## 4. Common VPN Problems and Solutions

### Problem 1: VPN Authentication Failed

**Possible causes:**
- Incorrect username or password.
- Expired password.
- Account temporarily locked.
- MFA verification failure.

**Solutions:**
1. Verify your username and password.
2. Check whether your company password has expired.
3. Complete MFA verification again.
4. If the issue continues, contact IT support.

### Problem 2: VPN Connection Timeout

**Possible causes:**
- Unstable internet connection.
- VPN server temporarily unavailable.
- Firewall blocking the VPN connection.

**Solutions:**
1. Check your internet connection.
2. Restart the VPN application.
3. Restart your computer.
4. Try connecting again.
5. If the issue continues, contact IT support.

### Problem 3: VPN Connected but Internal Resources Are Not Accessible

**Possible causes:**
- DNS resolution problems.
- Internal resource unavailable.
- Incorrect network configuration.

**Solutions:**
1. Disconnect and reconnect the VPN.
2. Restart the browser or application.
3. Verify that the internal resource is available.
4. Contact IT support if the issue persists.

## 5. Security Guidelines

- Never share your VPN credentials.
- Do not connect through untrusted VPN applications.
- Always disconnect the VPN when it is no longer required.
- Report suspicious login notifications to IT support.
- Never share MFA verification codes with anyone.

## 6. Escalation Procedure

If the above troubleshooting steps do not resolve the issue, contact the IT support team.

Include the following information:
- Employee ID.
- Device operating system.
- VPN application name.
- Exact error message.
- Approximate time the issue occurred.
- Troubleshooting steps already attempted.

## 7. Important Note

This document provides general VPN troubleshooting guidance. Actual VPN server addresses, approved client names, and organization-specific procedures must be obtained from the company's IT department.
