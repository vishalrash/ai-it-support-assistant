



Printers are used by employees to print documents, reports, forms, and other business materials.

This document explains how to connect a printer to a computer and troubleshoot common printing problems.

Employees should use only printers approved and configured by the IT department.



Before setting up a printer, ensure that:

- The printer is powered on.
- The printer has sufficient paper.
- The printer has sufficient ink or toner.
- The printer is connected to the office network or computer.
- You know the approved printer name or network address, if required.
- Your computer has the necessary printer driver.
- You have permission to use the printer.



Follow these general steps:

1. Turn on the printer.
2. Connect the printer to the computer or approved office network.
3. Open your computer's Settings.
4. Navigate to the Printers and Scanners section.
5. Select Add Printer or Scanner.
6. Wait for available printers to appear.
7. Select the approved office printer.
8. Follow the installation instructions.
9. Install the approved printer driver if required.
10. Print a test page to verify the connection.

**Important:** If the printer is not detected, contact IT support for the correct printer configuration.



To select a default printer:

1. Open Settings on your computer.
2. Navigate to Printers and Scanners.
3. Find the required printer.
4. Select the printer.
5. Choose Set as Default if the option is available.
6. Print a test page to verify the selection.

The exact steps may differ depending on the operating system.





**Possible causes:**
- Printer is powered off.
- USB or network connection is disconnected.
- Printer is not connected to the correct network.
- Printer driver is missing.
- Printer is unavailable.

**Solutions:**
1. Verify that the printer is powered on.
2. Check the USB or network connection.
3. Confirm that the printer is connected to the approved office network.
4. Restart the printer and computer.
5. Check whether the correct printer driver is installed.
6. Contact IT support if the printer is still not detected.



**Possible causes:**
- Previous print job failed.
- Printer is offline.
- Print spooler service has stopped.
- Printer communication problem.

**Solutions:**
1. Open the printer queue on your computer.
2. Check the status of pending print jobs.
3. Cancel a failed print job if permitted.
4. Restart the printer.
5. Try printing a test page.
6. Contact IT support if the queue remains stuck.



**Possible causes:**
- Printer is powered off.
- Network connection is unavailable.
- Printer is disconnected from the computer.
- Incorrect printer configuration.

**Solutions:**
1. Verify that the printer is powered on.
2. Check the printer's network connection.
3. Restart the printer.
4. Verify that your computer is connected to the correct network.
5. Check the printer status in Settings.
6. Contact IT support if the printer remains offline.



**Possible causes:**
- Paper loaded incorrectly.
- Damaged or folded paper.
- Too much paper in the tray.
- Foreign material inside the printer.

**Solutions:**
1. Turn off the printer if required by its instructions.
2. Open the appropriate printer access panel.
3. Carefully remove visible jammed paper.
4. Avoid pulling paper forcefully.
5. Check for remaining paper fragments.
6. Reload paper correctly.
7. Close all panels and restart the printer.
8. Contact IT support if the paper cannot be removed safely.



**Possible causes:**
- Low ink or toner.
- Incorrect paper type.
- Dirty printhead or printer components.
- Incorrect print settings.
- Damaged ink cartridge or toner unit.

**Solutions:**
1. Check ink or toner levels.
2. Verify that the correct paper is loaded.
3. Check print quality settings.
4. Run the printer's approved cleaning or maintenance procedure.
5. Replace consumables only through the approved process.
6. Contact IT support if print quality remains poor.



**Possible causes:**
- Printer is offline.
- Incorrect printer selected.
- Empty paper tray.
- Low ink or toner.
- Print job stuck in queue.
- Printer driver problem.

**Solutions:**
1. Confirm that the correct printer is selected.
2. Check whether the printer is powered on.
3. Verify that paper is available.
4. Check ink or toner levels.
5. Review the print queue for failed jobs.
6. Restart the printer and retry.
7. Contact IT support if printing still fails.



- Keep the printer in a clean and ventilated area.
- Use compatible paper.
- Avoid overfilling the paper tray.
- Replace ink or toner through the approved process.
- Keep printer firmware and drivers updated when instructed by IT.
- Follow manufacturer maintenance instructions.
- Report unusual noises, repeated jams, or hardware damage.



- Use only authorized office printers.
- Collect printed confidential documents immediately.
- Do not leave sensitive documents unattended.
- Do not connect unknown USB devices to office printers.
- Follow company policies when printing confidential information.
- Report suspicious printer activity to IT support.



If the above troubleshooting steps do not resolve the problem, contact the IT support team.

Provide the following information:

- Employee ID.
- Computer asset tag or identification number.
- Printer name or model.
- Printer location.
- Description of the issue.
- Exact error message, if available.
- Whether other employees are experiencing the same problem.
- Approximate time the issue started.
- Troubleshooting steps already attempted.
