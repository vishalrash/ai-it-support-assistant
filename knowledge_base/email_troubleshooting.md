



Company email is used for internal communication, sharing documents, receiving notifications, and coordinating business activities.

Employees must use their organization-approved email account and follow company security policies when accessing email services.



Before accessing company email, ensure that:

- You have an active company email account.
- You know your company email address and password.
- Your device has a stable internet connection.
- You have access to the approved email application or webmail portal.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is configured if required.



Follow these steps:

1. Open the organization's approved email application or webmail portal.
2. Enter your company email address.
3. Enter your account password.
4. Complete MFA verification if requested.
5. Wait for the inbox to load.
6. Verify that you can send and receive emails.

**Important:** Always access company email through the official portal or application provided by the IT department.





**Possible causes:**
- Incorrect username or password.
- Expired password.
- Account temporarily locked.
- MFA verification failure.
- Email service temporarily unavailable.

**Solutions:**
1. Verify your company email address.
2. Check that you are entering the correct password.
3. Confirm that your password has not expired.
4. Complete MFA verification again.
5. If the account remains inaccessible, contact IT support.



**Possible causes:**
- Emails are being filtered into spam or junk folders.
- Incorrect email filtering rules.
- Full mailbox storage.
- Sender entered an incorrect email address.
- Temporary email service interruption.

**Solutions:**
1. Check your spam and junk folders.
2. Search for the missing email using the sender's name or subject.
3. Review your inbox filtering rules.
4. Check whether your mailbox storage is full.
5. Ask the sender to verify the recipient address.
6. Contact IT support if the problem continues.



**Possible causes:**
- No internet connection.
- Incorrect recipient email address.
- Attachment exceeds the organization's size limit.
- Outgoing mail server problem.
- Mailbox restrictions.

**Solutions:**
1. Verify your internet connection.
2. Check the recipient's email address.
3. Try sending the message without large attachments.
4. Check whether the email is stuck in the Outbox.
5. Restart the email application.
6. Contact IT support if sending continues to fail.



**Possible causes:**
- Unstable internet connection.
- Application synchronization error.
- Outdated email application.
- Account authentication problem.

**Solutions:**
1. Check your internet connection.
2. Refresh the inbox.
3. Close and reopen the email application.
4. Verify that your account is still signed in.
5. Check for approved application updates.
6. Contact IT support if synchronization does not resume.



**Possible causes:**
- Unsupported file format.
- Corrupted attachment.
- Insufficient storage space.
- Missing application required to open the file.
- Security restrictions on the attachment.

**Solutions:**
1. Verify the attachment file format.
2. Download the attachment again.
3. Check whether the required application is installed.
4. Ask the sender to resend the attachment if it appears corrupted.
5. Contact IT support if a security restriction prevents access.



**Possible signs:**
- Unexpected requests for passwords or MFA codes.
- Unknown or suspicious sender addresses.
- Unusual links or attachments.
- Messages creating urgency or threatening account suspension.
- Requests for confidential company information.

**Solutions:**
1. Do not click suspicious links.
2. Do not download unexpected attachments.
3. Never share passwords or MFA verification codes.
4. Report the message using the organization's approved phishing-reporting process.
5. Contact IT support if you have already clicked a suspicious link or shared information.



- Never share your email password.
- Enable MFA if required by company policy.
- Avoid opening unexpected attachments.
- Verify suspicious sender addresses.
- Do not forward confidential company information to personal accounts.
- Lock your device when unattended.
- Report suspected phishing emails immediately.



If the above troubleshooting steps do not resolve the problem, contact the IT support team.

Provide the following information:

- Employee ID.
- Company email address.
- Email application or webmail portal being used.
- Description of the issue.
- Exact error message, if available.
- Approximate time the issue started.
- Troubleshooting steps already attempted.

Never include your password or MFA verification code in a support request.



This document provides general email troubleshooting guidance. The official email portal, supported applications, mailbox storage limits, attachment size limits, and organization-specific security policies must be obtained from the IT department.
