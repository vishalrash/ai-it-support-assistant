



This document explains how employees can reset or change their company account passwords.

Employees should follow the approved password recovery process to maintain account security and prevent unauthorized access.



A password reset may be required when:

- You have forgotten your password.
- Your password has expired.
- Your account has been locked after multiple unsuccessful login attempts.
- You suspect that someone else knows your password.
- You receive a password expiration notification.



Follow these steps to reset your company account password:

1. Open the organization's approved password management portal.
2. Select the Forgot Password or Reset Password option.
3. Enter your registered company email address or username.
4. Complete the identity verification process.
5. Follow the password reset instructions provided by the portal.
6. Create a new password that meets the organization's password policy.
7. Confirm the new password.
8. Sign in again using the new password.

**Important:** Use only the official password management portal provided by your IT department.



When creating a new password:

- Follow the minimum length specified by your organization's password policy.
- Use a combination of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters where required.
- Avoid easily guessed information such as your name, birthday, or username.
- Do not reuse old or compromised passwords.
- Never share your password with another person.

The exact password length and complexity requirements must be confirmed with the organization's IT department.

1. Verify that you entered the correct company email address.

**Possible causes:**
- Multiple unsuccessful login attempts.
- Suspicious login activity.
- Account security restrictions.

**Solutions:**
1. Stop attempting to log in repeatedly.
2. Follow the organization's account recovery procedure.
3. Complete identity verification if requested.
4. Contact IT support if the account remains locked.



**Possible causes:**
- Password does not meet complexity requirements.
- Password is too short.
- Password matches a previously used password.
- Password contains restricted information.

**Solutions:**
1. Review the organization's password requirements.
2. Create a new password that meets the requirements.
3. Avoid reusing previous passwords.
4. Try resetting the password again.



**Possible causes:**
- Incorrect password entered.
- Old credentials stored in an application.
- Password synchronization delay.

**Solutions:**
1. Verify that you are using the new password.
2. Sign out and sign in again.
3. Update saved credentials in applications where necessary.
4. Contact IT support if the issue continues.

- Do not use the same password for personal and company accounts.
- Avoid saving passwords on shared computers.
- Always use the official password reset portal.
- Report suspected unauthorized account access immediately.
- Do not approve unexpected MFA requests.



If the password reset process fails, contact the IT support team.

Provide the following information:

- Employee ID or company username.
- Registered company email address.
- Exact error message.
- Approximate time the issue occurred.
- Whether the account is locked.
- Troubleshooting steps already attempted.

Never send your current password or MFA verification code to IT support.
